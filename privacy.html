Privacy Policy

Triassic Tiles · Last updated: June 7, 2026

Summary

Triassic Tiles does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The game runs entirely on your device.

What information the game stores

Triassic Tiles saves the following on your device using your browser's standard local storage:

  • Your current game board and score
  • Your best score
  • Which dinosaurs you've unlocked in the Dino Dex
  • Whether you've dismissed the "How to Play" tutorial
  • Whether sound is muted

This information stays on your device. It is not sent to any server, the developer, or any third party.

What we do not collect

Triassic Tiles does not collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any other identifying information
  • Your location
  • Your contacts, photos, files, or anything else on your device
  • Analytics, telemetry, or usage data
  • Advertising identifiers

Third-party services

Triassic Tiles does not use third-party services, SDKs, analytics, or advertising networks.

Network use

The game is loaded over the network on first visit. After that, it works fully offline. No network requests are made during gameplay.

Children's privacy

Triassic Tiles is appropriate for all ages. Because no personal information is collected, the game's privacy practices are the same for children as for any other player.

Your control over data

You can delete all data the game has stored at any time by clearing your browser's site data for the domain hosting the game, or by uninstalling the app from your device.

Changes to this policy

If the game's data practices ever change — for example, if advertising is added in a future version — this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change.
